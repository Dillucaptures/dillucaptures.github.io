ABOUT ME

I photograph the space between moments.

I’m Dillu, a photographer drawn to honest expressions, cinematic light, and photographs that still feel personal years later. From intimate portraits to celebrations and editorial stories, I build each frame around the people inside it.

My approach is simple: observe first, direct gently, and let the image breathe. The final photograph should feel polished without losing the texture of the real moment.
