About the Photographer

Quiet moments, honestly told

I'm a photographer based in India, working worldwide. My approach is patient and observational: natural light, unposed emotion and compositions that give a scene room to breathe.

My work has appeared in travel magazines, gallery exhibitions and private collections. Whether it is a mountain at dawn or a wedding at dusk, I look for the frame that feels like the memory.
